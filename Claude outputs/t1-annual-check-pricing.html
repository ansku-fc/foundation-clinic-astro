Tier 1 annual health check — pricing options

Decision memo for the Medical Director · Foundation Clinic · Sep 2026 · new (hourly) doctor-comp model

RECOMMENDATION

Include the annual check as a member benefit at a discounted co-pay of ~€149 — not free at T1. A free check cuts T1 clinical margin from ~78% to ~57% today and, more importantly, removes the low-utilization cushion that makes T1 profitable. A €149 co-pay covers most of the ~€251 marginal cost, still reads as a strong perk (à la carte ≈ €300+), and keeps T1 above the 45% target across any realistic utilization. Reserve a genuinely free check for T2 (€199), where the fee absorbs it — and use it as the T1→T2 upsell.

When does rising pool usage force a change?

T1's ~78% margin exists only because members underuse their 20-min pool — today they average 9.6 min/month (48%), so ~87% aren't using their full pool. The chart shows T1 gross margin as that average utilization climbs. The horizontal line is the 45% clinic target; where a curve crosses it is the point to act (move the member to T2, or raise the co-pay).

À la carte Co-pay €149 Co-pay €99 Free check 45% target

Free check: stays above the 45% target until average pool use reaches ~79% (~16 of 20 min). In plain terms — act when the share not using their full pool falls to about 35% (i.e. ~65% of T1 members maxing out).

€99 co-pay: viable to ~90% utilization; €149 co-pay: to ~96% — i.e. any realistic level.

Fully utilization-proof (≥45% even at 100% pool use) needs a co-pay of ~€185; à la carte is proof by construction.

Why not free at T1

  • It's really T2 economics. To give a free check and hold today's ~78% margin, T1 would need to be priced at ~€193/month — i.e. T2.
  • It removes the deductible on our most expensive service. Today T1 is cheap because most members use little. A free check is a guaranteed €251 event every taker consumes — you trade a favourable "most use little" spread for a fixed high cost.
  • Induced demand. Annual checks surface findings → follow-up visits → more doctor time, eroding margin beyond the €213.
